Reconciles the 18 engineering bounded contexts (E 2.4) and shared or cross-cutting engineering rules against the commercial master.
The master is authoritative for commercial matters. Conflicts are logged and routed to an approver, not resolved silently.
Proposed decisions PD-11..PD-15 are defined in [02_Commercial_Decision_Register.md](02_Commercial_Decision_Register.md).

**Coverage:** 18 of 18 contexts (CTX-01..CTX-18), plus 9 shared or cross-cutting rows.
**Status counts:** Aligned 8, Gap 14, Inconsistency 5.
